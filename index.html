Release Agent

Release Agent is a private, personal tool used only by the owner of this page (an independent music artist) to publish her own music videos to her own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy Policy

  • The tool connects only to the owner's own accounts, and only with her explicit permission.
  • It uploads videos that she created and approved. It does not read, collect, store, sell or share any personal data of other people.
  • Access tokens are stored privately as encrypted secrets and are used only to upload her videos.
  • No analytics, cookies or tracking are used.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account settings (Security → Third-party access) or in the TikTok app settings.

Terms of Service

  • Release Agent is provided for the personal use of its owner only.
  • Content is published only to accounts owned by the owner and only after her approval.
  • Use of each platform remains subject to that platform's own terms (YouTube Terms of Service, TikTok Terms of Service, Meta Terms).
  • The tool is provided as is, without warranties.
